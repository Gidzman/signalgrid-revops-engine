# Project Requirements & Business Context: SignalGrid

## 1. Company Profile
- **Company Name:** CloudScale B2B SaaS
- **Product:** Enterprise Cloud Infrastructure Monitoring & Observability Platform
- **Target Market:** Mid-Market and Enterprise (50–500+ employees)
- **Tech Stack Focus:** AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, Microservices

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Company Size:** 50 to 500 employees
- **Industry:** SaaS, Fintech, HealthTech, Enterprise Software
- **Key Decision Makers:** CTO, VP of Engineering, Director of DevOps, Infrastructure Leads
- **Buying Triggers:** Rapid scaling, multi-region cloud outages, Kubernetes cluster sprawl, compliance demands

## 3. Core Revenue Operations Problems Addressed
1. Incomplete/outdated company and contact records from mixed inbound/outbound lists.
2. Conflicting firmographic data from multiple enrichment vendors.
3. Manual research overhead slowing down sales development reps (SDRs).
4. Silent API/webhook integration failures corrupting HubSpot CRM fields.
5. Unverified email addresses damaging domain reputation during outreach.
